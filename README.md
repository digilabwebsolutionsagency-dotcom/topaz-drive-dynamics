# Topaz Transport Hub

Create a highly professional, modern, responsive, and high-converting single-page website with dynamic sub-page modals/sections for "TOPAZ Transport CC", a leading logistics, transport, and heavy plant hire company based in Windhoek, Namibia.

---

### Visual Style & Theme Guidelines
- Color Palette: 
  - Primary Accent: Industrial Yellow / Topaz Gold (`#EAB308` or `#D97706` derived from company branding)
  - Dark Neutral: Charcoal Black / Deep Slate (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`)
  - Light Neutral: Off-white / Light Concrete (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Accent/Status: Clean White and Vibrant Green indicator badges.
- Typography: Clean, bold, industrial sans-serif headers (e.g., Oswald, Inter, or Montserrat) paired with readable body text.
- Tone: Trustworthy, authoritative, innovative, heavy-duty, and premium.
- Aesthetic Components: High-contrast call-to-action (CTA) buttons, subtle backdrop blurs, crisp grid layouts for fleet equipment, glassmorphism cards, and interactive quote request forms.

---

### Header & Navigation
- Logo Text: "TOPAZ TRANSPORT" with the subtitle "logistics + plant hire"
- Quick Contact Ribbon: Display phone (+264 61 401 411 / +264 81 141 1114) and primary email (info@topazinv.com) prominently at the very top.
- Navigation Links: Home, About Us, Services, Fleet & Equipment, Why Choose Us, Contact Us.
- Header CTA Button: "Get a Quote" (Primary Yellow button with smooth auto-scroll to the contact form).
